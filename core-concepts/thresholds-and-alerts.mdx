# Thresholds and Alert Rules

Thresholds are the boundaries Senzo uses to mark a metric as normal, warning or critical. There are two kinds, and they do different jobs:

- **Standard thresholds** are built into Senzo. They can't be changed for an organization.
- **Alert rules** belong to your organization. They decide which alerts are raised. They don't change the standard thresholds.

## Standard thresholds

| Metric | Warning | Critical |
|---|---|---|
| Overtime Rate | 10% | 15% |
| Vacancy Rate | 8% | 20% |
| Absence Rate | 5% | 8% |
| Agency Rate | 5% | 10% |
| OT Cost % of Labor | 20% | 30% |
| Agency Cost % of Labor | 15% | 25% |
| Outflow Rate | 8% | 15% |

These values drive Pulse Intelligence findings, the Burnout Risk Index, the Overview (including Emerging Risks and Top Actions), the trend charts, Metrics Explorer, Data Explorer and Cost Analytics.

## Alert rules

Admins manage alert rules on the [Alert Rules](/features/alert-rules) page. Each rule watches one metric (Overtime %, Sick Time %, Vacancy FTE, NHPPD or NPP) in one scope, and raises an alert of the chosen severity when the value is above, below or outside the range you set.

Senzo checks every enabled rule once a day against your most recent data. An alert closes automatically when its condition clears.

Alerts appear on the Alert Rules page, in Pulse Intelligence (Active Alerts and Recent Alerts), on the Unit Health page, in data exports and in the weekly digest email.
